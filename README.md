### 🧩 Research Interests

* **Multi-Camera BEV & 3D Perception**

  * 3D object detection
  * BEV segmentation
  * Camera-to-BEV view transformation

* **Robust & Geometry-aware Perception**

  * Camera viewpoint and pose changes
  * Calibration mismatch
  * Cross-platform sensor configuration
  * Domain and distribution shifts

* **LiDAR 3D Perception**

  * Unsupervised / self-supervised 3D object detection
  * Object discovery and temporal consistency
  * LiDAR-based autonomous driving perception

* **Real-world Autonomous Driving Systems**

  * Camera / LiDAR calibration
  * Multi-sensor synchronization
  * Embedded AI deployment
  * Reliability under sensor and system failures

---

### 🔬 What I’m Interested In

I am particularly interested in understanding **why autonomous-driving perception models fail when deployed outside their training conditions**.

Rather than focusing only on benchmark performance, I am interested in how changes in **camera geometry, sensor configuration, calibration, and vehicle platforms** affect perception systems, and how these failures can be diagnosed and mitigated.

My long-term research goal is to develop **autonomous-driving systems that remain reliable across different vehicles, sensor configurations, and real-world operating conditions**.
