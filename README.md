## About me

I am an undergraduate researcher at the [IRCV Lab](https://ircv.hanyang.ac.kr/), Hanyang University, advised by Prof. Soonmin Hwang, and will begin my M.S. in Automotive Engineering there in March 2027.

My research focuses on Robust Spatial Intelligence for Autonomous Driving. My goal is to build robust 3D perception and spatial reasoning systems that generalize across diverse environments and sensor configurations.

Before AI research, I designed and analyzed race cars with Hanyang's Formula Student Team RACE, which taught me to build systems that work on real vehicles, not just in theory.

## Interests

- Robust BEV & 3D Perception
- Sensor Fusion
- End-to-End Driving
- Spatial Reasoning
