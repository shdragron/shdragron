## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an undergraduate researcher at the **IRCV Lab, Hanyang University**, working on
**computer vision and autonomous driving**.

My research focuses on understanding and improving the reliability of autonomous-driving
systems under changes in **geometry, sensors, environments, and vehicle platforms**.

Rather than optimizing only for benchmark performance, I am particularly interested in
understanding **what autonomous-driving models actually learn, why they fail, and how their
spatial reasoning can be made more robust in real-world deployment**.

My research spans both **vision-based perception and decision-making**, with an emphasis on
connecting learning-based methods with the physical geometry of autonomous vehicles.

---

## 🔬 Research Interests

### 🚗 Robust Autonomous Driving
- Robustness under camera viewpoint and sensor configuration changes
- Calibration errors and cross-platform deployment
- Distribution shifts and failure analysis
- Causal analysis of autonomous-driving models

### 🌐 3D & BEV Perception
- Multi-camera BEV perception
- 3D object detection
- Camera-to-BEV view transformation
- Geometry-aware representation learning

### 📡 LiDAR & Spatial Intelligence
- LiDAR-based 3D perception
- Metric 3D grounding and localization
- Object discovery and temporal reasoning
- Multi-modal spatial reasoning

### 🧠 End-to-End Driving
- Robust end-to-end autonomous driving
- Counterfactual and causal model analysis
- Decision-aware scene understanding
- Perception-to-planning reliability

### 🏎️ Real-World Autonomous Systems
- Camera / LiDAR calibration
- Multi-sensor synchronization
- Embedded AI deployment
- Autonomous vehicle integration and testing

---

## 🧭 Research Direction

> **Metric Spatial Intelligence for Robust Autonomous Driving**

I am interested in building autonomous-driving systems that not only recognize the world,
but also understand **where things are, what matters for driving decisions, and how those
relationships change under real-world perturbations**.

My long-term goal is to develop models that maintain reliable spatial reasoning and
decision-making across different **vehicles, sensors, viewpoints, and operating conditions**.
